# ASCII tree portfolio

Green ASCII foliage, warm gold data garlands, white highlights and distinct technology colors against deep navy #080d18.

The hero pairs identity with a large rotating tree. Seventeen technology fruit share its 3D rotation; hover and focus reveal names. Three brand-headed AI workers sit on the left foreground, typing and sending small Git-story packets to the tree.

ASCII roots continue into a semantic chronological timeline. The centre activation band triggers a short electrical arrival and bright green powered state. Study and project branches fork off the trunk like a Git graph; experience and education follow as two short rows.

Space Grotesk carries identity/headings; IBM Plex Mono and Consolas carry code/data/ASCII. Fonts are self-hosted. Animations share pause/offscreen behavior and respect reduced motion.

Mobile places the name above the tree, switches to a single-sided timeline and keeps the three AI workers together. Retained legacy case-study URLs keep their original styles/scripts.
